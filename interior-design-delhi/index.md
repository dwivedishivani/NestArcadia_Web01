# Interior Design in Delhi

Residential and commercial interior design in Delhi with a focus on practical layouts, custom furniture, materials, lighting and a distinctly Indian sense of place.

Design with a point of view

## Modern Indian interiors, without losing the human part.

Delhi homes can range from compact apartments to larger independent properties, each carrying different spatial and material realities. NestArcadia creates a clear design direction around the client's lifestyle, the architecture and what needs to work every day.

## Planning considerations for your space

### Respect the architecture

Existing proportions, light, circulation and structural realities shape the design rather than being ignored.

### Make storage deliberate

Custom storage is used where it solves a real problem, not simply because an empty wall is available.

### Bring craft in carefully

Indian materials, textures and details can create identity without turning the home into a theme.
