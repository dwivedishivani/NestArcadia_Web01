Frequently asked questions

What type of projects do you take in Noida?

Residential apartments, villas and selected commercial or office interiors, depending on project scope and requirements.

Can you help with both design and execution?

Yes. The engagement can cover design only or extend through execution coordination and handover.

How much does a 2BHK interior design cost in Noida?

For a raw or newly handed-over 2BHK, NestArcadia's published starting guidance is around ₹6.5–7 lakh. This covers space planning, modular kitchen, wardrobes, false ceiling, electrical finishing, lighting, furniture and final handover. Costs change with possession status, material choices and scope.

How much does a 3BHK interior design cost in Noida?

A 3BHK typically starts around ₹9–10 lakh for a raw flat. Larger scope, premium materials, full custom furniture or extensive décor can increase the total. The best way to get an accurate figure is to share your floor plan.

Do you work on 4BHK and villa interiors in Noida?

Yes. 4BHK apartments and villas are quoted based on the floor plan, number of rooms, finish level and turnkey scope. Share your property details to start the conversation.
