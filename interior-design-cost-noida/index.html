Interior Design Cost in Noida: 2BHK, 3BHK & 4BHK

How much does interior design cost in Noida? NestArcadia's honest guide to 2BHK, 3BHK and 4BHK interior budgets in Noida, Greater Noida West and NCR — with real INR starting figures.

An honest cost guide

What does interior design actually cost in Noida and NCR?

The most common question before a project begins is: how much will this cost? The answer depends on the property, the scope and the finish level — but there are reliable starting points. This page sets out what NestArcadia's clients have paid for 2BHK, 3BHK and 4BHK interiors in Noida, Greater Noida West and Noida Extension, and explains the factors that push budgets up or down.

Planning considerations for your space

2BHK interior design cost in Noida

A complete 2BHK interior for a raw or newly handed-over flat typically ranges from ₹5–12 lakh. This covers modular kitchen, wardrobes for two bedrooms, false ceiling with lighting, electrical finishing, flooring, furniture selection and final handover. Premium materials, full custom furniture or extensive decor can push this higher.

3BHK interior design cost in Noida

A 3BHK complete interior typically ranges from ₹7–22 lakh. Three bedrooms of storage, a full kitchen, false ceiling across common areas, lighting, flooring and coordination are included in a typical scope. The number moves based on the number of custom pieces, material choices and site condition.

4BHK and villa interior design cost

A 4BHK apartment typically starts at ₹14–17 lakh depending on floor area, finish level and the number of custom pieces. Villa and farmhouse projects are quoted on the floor plan and agreed scope — the per-square-foot figure is less useful here because room character, ceiling heights and outdoor areas vary significantly.

What changes the final cost?

The possession status (raw vs partly finished), material tier (standard laminates vs veneer vs lacquer), ratio of modular to custom furniture, extent of false ceiling, lighting fixtures and amount of decor are the main cost variables. Sharing the floor plan and a rough scope is the fastest way to get a realistic number.

Frequently asked questions

How much does 2BHK interior design cost in Noida?

A complete 2BHK interior in Noida, covering modular kitchen, wardrobes, false ceiling, lighting, electrical finishing and furniture, typically ranges from ₹5–12 lakh. If civil or electrical work is partly done, the cost may sit at the lower end of the range. Premium materials and custom furniture can increase it.

How much does 3BHK interior design cost in Noida?

A full 3BHK interior typically ranges from ₹7–22 lakh in Noida and Greater Noida West. This includes three bedrooms of storage, a complete kitchen, false ceiling across common areas, lighting and flooring. The final number depends on material tier and how much is custom-made.

What is included in a turnkey interior package?

A turnkey interior typically includes the design scope (space planning, 3D visualisation, working drawings), material selection and procurement, modular furniture (kitchen, wardrobes, storage units), civil finishing, false ceiling, electrical and lighting work, and final handover with a site review.

Can I get a 2BHK done under ₹5 lakh?

A complete 2BHK interior including kitchen, wardrobes, false ceiling, lighting, flooring and furniture is difficult to do well under ₹5 lakh in Noida today, so ₹5 lakh is our minimum project budget. A standard scope with mid-range materials usually falls within ₹5–12 lakh. A design-only engagement or a partial scope (kitchen and wardrobes only) can be discussed if the full budget is not available yet.

Does NestArcadia charge for the design separately?

The design is typically part of the overall project scope rather than a standalone charge. For clients who want design documentation only — drawings, material specs and BOQ without execution — a focused design engagement can be discussed.
