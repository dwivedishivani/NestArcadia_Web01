# Interior Design Cost in Noida: 2BHK ₹5–12L, 3BHK ₹7–22L — FAQs

2BHK interior ₹5–12L, 3BHK ₹7–22L in Noida & Greater Noida West. Plain answers on cost, timeline, turnkey execution, modular kitchens and Vastu — no jargon.

## Working with NestArcadia

**Which areas does NestArcadia serve?**

NestArcadia's primary service focus is Noida Extension and Greater Noida West, with nearby Noida and Greater Noida as secondary markets. For projects elsewhere in NCR, availability is assessed based on the property's location and scope.

**Do you provide turnkey interior design and execution?**

Yes. Depending on the project, we can manage the process from planning and design through materials, site execution and final handover.

**When should I contact an interior designer?**

Ideally, before possession or before major civil and electrical work begins. Planning early gives you more flexibility with kitchen layouts, wardrobes, storage, lighting and electrical points.

## Homes, layouts and budgets

**Do you design 2BHK, 3BHK and 4BHK interiors?**

Yes. We work on 2BHK, 3BHK and 4BHK apartments, villas and farmhouses, with the design adapted to the layout, carpet area, storage needs and level of customisation.

**How much does a 2BHK or 3BHK interior cost in Greater Noida West?**

For a raw or newly handed-over apartment, a complete interior project typically ranges from ₹5–12 lakh for a 2BHK and ₹7–22 lakh for a 3BHK. If flooring, electrical or some civil work is already completed, the cost may sit at the lower end of the range. More customised furniture, premium materials or extensive décor can increase the budget depending on the scope.

**Can you plan space-saving interiors for a compact Noida apartment?**

Yes. We focus on practical circulation, well-planned storage and furniture that fits the actual space, so the home feels useful without feeling overcrowded.

## Services and next steps

**Do you offer Vastu-friendly interior planning?**

Yes. If Vastu is important to you, we can consider relevant principles alongside practical layout, daylight, movement, storage and everyday functionality.

**How do I begin an interior design project with NestArcadia?**

Share your property type, location, possession timeline and what you need from the space. We can then guide you on the right next step.
