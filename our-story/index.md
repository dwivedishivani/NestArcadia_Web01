# Our Story | Ancient Craft, Modern Indian Interiors

Meet NestArcadia, an interior design studio translating India's material heritage into homes made for modern living.

## A design approach rooted in how people live

NestArcadia brings Indian material heritage and contemporary interior planning together. The starting point is not a style label; it is the way a household uses its rooms, stores everyday things, moves through the home and spends time together.

Craft, texture and regional references add character when they are used with intention. Space planning, lighting, storage and execution details make that character work in daily life.

## From first brief to finished space

- Understand the property, floor plan, routines and priorities
- Resolve layout, furniture scale, storage and key services
- Develop the design direction, materials and execution details
- Coordinate the agreed work and review the finished space

## Design for homes across Noida and NCR

NestArcadia focuses on residential and commercial interior requirements across Noida Extension, Greater Noida West, Noida and nearby NCR locations. Each project begins with its own site conditions, brief and agreed scope.
