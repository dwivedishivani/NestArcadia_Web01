Explore NestArcadia's selected interior design projects and residential design studies across India.

## Selected home concepts and interior design studies

Explore a selection of interior design directions for apartments, villas and family homes. The examples illustrate how material palettes, furniture scale, natural light, storage and regional references can shape a coherent living space.

The examples are presented as design studies, showing possible design directions rather than claiming every concept is a completed installation.

## What to look for in a well-planned interior

- A layout that protects comfortable movement
- Storage planned around actual household routines
- Materials chosen for appearance, durability and maintenance
- Lighting coordinated with furniture and daily activities
- Details that connect the rooms into one consistent design
