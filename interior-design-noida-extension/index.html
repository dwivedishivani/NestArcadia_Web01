Designing for apartment life

Start with the floor plan. Then make it feel like home.

Noida Extension is largely an apartment-led market, which means good interiors have to respect the realities of the builder plan. A mood board can establish a direction, but it cannot tell you whether a wardrobe will steal circulation, whether the sofa is the right scale, or whether the kitchen has enough usable storage. Our design process brings those practical decisions forward, then builds the visual language around them.

Planning considerations for your space

Make every square foot work

Compact rooms benefit from deliberate furniture sizes, vertical storage and clear circulation. The goal is not to fill the apartment; it is to make it easier to live in.

Design around real routines

Work-from-home, guests, children, appliances and everyday storage all compete for space. We plan the home around how your household actually moves through it.

Plan before the site gets busy

Drawings, materials, lighting and service decisions are easier to coordinate before execution starts. That creates a clearer brief for the people building the work.

Match the finish to the budget

The starting budget depends on the condition of the apartment and what is included. For newly handed-over homes, NestArcadia's published guidance starts around ₹6.5–7 lakh for a 2BHK and ₹9–10 lakh for a 3BHK, before adjustments for customisation and scope.

Our process

  1. Understand the property — review drawings, site conditions, household requirements and budget direction.
  2. Plan the space — define room roles, circulation, storage and fixed-service requirements.
  3. Develop the design — visualise the palette and document the details needed for execution.
  4. Coordinate execution — manage the sequence of civil, electrical, ceiling, joinery and finish work.
  5. Review handover — check the finished space for function, fit and agreed scope.

Frequently asked questions

Can you design a compact 2BHK in Noida Extension?

Yes. Compact homes benefit from early space planning, especially around entry storage, kitchen workflow, wardrobes, flexible rooms and furniture scale.

How much does a 2BHK or 3BHK interior cost in Noida Extension?

For a raw or newly handed-over apartment, NestArcadia's published starting guidance is around ₹6.5–7 lakh for a 2BHK and ₹9–10 lakh for a 3BHK. Completed flooring, electrical or civil work can change the cost; premium materials, custom furniture and extensive decor can increase it. A detailed estimate needs the floor plan and agreed scope.

Do you work with new-possession apartments?

Yes. Early involvement can help coordinate layout, electrical, lighting, storage and material decisions before execution begins.

Do you provide only design, or execution too?

The scope can be tailored. You can discuss focused design support or a broader turnkey process depending on the project requirements.
