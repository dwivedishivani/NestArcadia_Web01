| "interior designer Noida" vs "interior designer Noida Extension" | `/interior-design-noida` vs `/interior-design-noida-extension` | Differentiate content clearly — Noida Extension page focuses on high-rise new builds, Noida page on the broader city |

Landing page = commercial (hire us for 2BHK). Different intent — no cannibalisation if content differentiated |
