## A new home deserves more than a package of finishes

Greater Noida West has grown around apartment living, new possessions and fast-changing family needs. The interior brief is rarely just about how the home should look. It is about where the dining table can actually sit, how much storage a family needs, whether the kitchen works every day, and which electrical and lighting decisions should be made before the walls are finished. We start there — with the property and the people who will use it.

## Planning considerations for your space

### New-possession planning

The best time to resolve kitchen layouts, wardrobes, lighting and electrical points is before execution gathers speed. Early planning reduces avoidable changes later.

### Storage without crowding

Apartment interiors need storage, but not at the cost of circulation. We look at full-height opportunities, furniture scale and what needs to stay accessible every day.

### A home that feels personal

A local interior should still feel like your home, not a model apartment. Materials, colour, furniture and details are developed around your routines and preferences.

### Set a realistic scope before execution

For a raw or newly handed-over apartment, NestArcadia's published guidance ranges from ₹5–12 lakh for a 2BHK and ₹7–22 lakh for a 3BHK. The final quote depends on what is already complete, the storage and furniture scope, materials and customisation.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do you design 2BHK and 3BHK interiors in Greater Noida West?**

Yes. NestArcadia plans interiors for 2BHK, 3BHK and 4BHK apartments, with the scope shaped around the actual layout, household routines, storage needs and material choices.

**What is the typical 2BHK or 3BHK interior design budget in Greater Noida West?**

For a raw or newly handed-over apartment, NestArcadia's published guidance ranges from ₹5–12 lakh for a 2BHK and ₹7–22 lakh for a 3BHK. If flooring, electrical or some civil work is already complete, the cost may sit at the lower end of the range; premium materials, custom furniture and extensive decor can increase it. The property and exact scope need to be reviewed for a quote.

**Can you handle turnkey execution in Greater Noida West?**

Depending on the project scope, turnkey work can include design development, material coordination, custom and modular furniture, site coordination, lighting and final handover.

**When should I contact an interior designer for a new-possession flat?**

Ideally before civil work and major service decisions begin. Early planning gives the design team more control over electrical points, kitchen planning, storage, lighting and sequencing.
