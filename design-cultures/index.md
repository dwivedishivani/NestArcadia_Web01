# Indian Design Cultures for Modern Homes

Discover how North, South, East, West and Central Indian design traditions shape contemporary homes in Noida, Greater Noida and NCR.

## Regional inspiration, interpreted for modern homes

Indian interiors are shaped by climate, craft, materials and the ways families use their homes. NestArcadia draws on regional design languages as a source of inspiration, then adapts the details to contemporary layouts and everyday needs.

Explore ideas influenced by North, South, East, West and Central India — from warm wood and woven textures to colour, carved details, natural finishes and thoughtful light.

## Design details we consider

- Material and finish combinations that suit the room and its use
- Craft-inspired screens, woodwork, textiles and surface details
- Light, ventilation, furniture scale and circulation
- A balanced mix of heritage references and contemporary function

## Bring an idea into your own floor plan

A reference image is a starting point, not a ready-made solution. We consider the dimensions, natural light, storage needs and maintenance expectations of your property before translating an inspiration into a practical interior.
