# Privacy Policy

NestArcadia privacy policy covering website data, analytics, advertising, enquiries, cookies and third-party services.

## What this policy covers

NestArcadia respects your privacy. This notice explains what information may be collected through nestarcadia.com, why it is used, the external services involved (including Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Supabase and WhatsApp links), and the choices available to you.

## Your choices

- Analytics and advertising storage are denied by default and change only when you make a privacy choice
- You can reopen Privacy Choices from the control at the bottom-left of the website
- You can ask NestArcadia what information it holds, or request correction or deletion, at info@nestarcadia.com
