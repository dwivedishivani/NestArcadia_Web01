# Interior Design Services in Noida, Greater Noida & NCR

Residential and commercial interior design, turnkey execution, custom furniture, Vastu-led planning and decor for Noida, Greater Noida West, Noida Extension, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, Delhi and Gurugram.

## Residential interior design

Plan 2BHK, 3BHK and 4BHK apartments, villas and family homes around the floor plan, household routines, storage requirements and budget. The design scope can include furniture layouts, kitchens, wardrobes, lighting, materials and decor.

## Commercial interiors

Create functional offices and commercial spaces with considered layouts for reception areas, workstations, meeting rooms, storage, lighting and acoustics. The scope is tailored to the business, site conditions and project brief.

## Design and execution services

- Space planning and furniture layouts
- Modular kitchens and custom furniture
- Wardrobes and integrated storage
- Vastu-led layout discussions when requested
- Lighting, materials and decor
- Turnkey execution coordination from design through handover
